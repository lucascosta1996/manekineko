Structure before color

A shape worth remembering.

Four exploratory directions for Tincta and Rewards, informed by the clarity of Morph and Assembly. Judge the silhouette first, then the internal construction. The circular previews let us see what survives at profile-picture size.

AIncision

CONTOUR / SCULPTURAL

A deliberate cut through a continuous field. The closest relative to Morph, with a more irregular outline and an open seam that gives the eye a feature to remember.

Next refinement: fewer, wider-spaced contours for the smallest avatar; preserve the incision and stepped shoulder.

BCounterfold

FOLDED / DIRECTIONAL

Two planes, one decisive crossing. Parallel tracks form an angular fan and a bowed return. The revised sketch is more compact and avoids the initial S-like trajectory.

Next refinement: give the crossing more space and reduce the unequal fans into a clearer small-size silhouette.

CCellwork

MODULAR / BOLD

Separate pieces, one memorable whole. Related to Assembly’s modular logic, with unequal masses and a continuous white channel. The strongest starting point for a small PFP.

Next refinement: widen the finest seams and tune each cell so the overall shape feels intentional at every scale.

Open full sketch ↗

DBranchform

GENERATIVE / ORGANIC

One rule, several possible outcomes. A more organic direction with an unequal, open silhouette. The branching rhythm makes its construction easy to follow.

Next refinement: make it more compact, simplify the thin tracks and reduce the literal tree-like reading.

Suggested shortlist

A for sculptural character.
C for the clearest PFP.

My starting pair would be Incision for Tincta and Cellwork for Rewards. Both can belong beside Morph and Assembly while giving each project a different silhouette. This is a proposed pairing, not a selection.

After you choose

Refine one direction into three close variants, then draw precise vector masters and a simpler avatar version. Review the square and circular compositions in black and white before introducing color. These are generated concept sketches; tiny joins and line spacing are still provisional.
