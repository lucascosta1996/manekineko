Recommendation

B communicates the reward more directly.

Award is my lead when immediate prize recognition is the priority. Unfold has a more exploratory art character. Both restore detail and rhythm while giving each fold a purpose within a larger readable shape.

Verify the association

Show an unlabeled image briefly to someone unfamiliar with the study. Ask what it resembles and what they think it belongs to, before mentioning rewards. Repeat at avatar size. This is a qualitative check of first impressions, not proof of a universal subconscious response.
