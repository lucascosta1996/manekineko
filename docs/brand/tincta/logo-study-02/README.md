## Brief and recommendation

The current compact lowercase t invites a Tumblr association. The request is to research DeFi, centralized and decentralized exchanges and adjacent financial identities, then propose a unique, refined, clean and modern direction with the discipline of an established corporate identity.

**Recommended starting point: A / Aperture.** Its two offset forms describe an open field rather than a letter. This connects to Tincta's existing geometric visual language and its parent-brand relationship between defined structures and participation. It can support Rewards without becoming a reward, coin, ticket or trading icon.

This recommendation is a design judgment, not a measured consumer preference or a claim of trademark availability. The three marks are original drawings; the wordmarks use disclosed, licensed type sources with the modifications described below. A selection should precede final refinement and app adoption.

## Findings and design criteria

1. **One memorable silhouette.** Recognizable corporate identities often reduce to one repeatable visual decision. Tincta should be identifiable without its color, animation or wordmark. A common shape alone is not enough; proportions and negative space must distinguish it.
2. **An internally related pair.** Symbol and lettering should share a weight, terminal treatment or structural idea. Avoid attaching a generic font to an unrelated icon.
3. **A neutral parent, expressive content.** The existing geometric artwork and exact collection palettes already provide variety. The logo should remain stable around them.
4. **Optical care.** Small sizes, white-on-black reproduction and tight counters need separate review. The study includes provisional micro drawings; it does not pretend that a reduced large mark is a final favicon.
5. **A durable family.** Parent Tincta, Rewards and any later project need a coherent hierarchy. A literal prize or ticket would over-specialize the parent identity.
6. **A recognizable name.** Tincta should remain easy to read. Keep ordinary written casing as “Tincta” even if a selected vector wordmark uses lowercase or uppercase.

The crowded territories are hollow hexagons, nested diamonds, block-grid crosses, exchange arrows, interlocking rings, banded planets and detailed mascots. The study learns from their consistency while seeking another form.

## The options

| Route | Character | Lettering | Best reason to pursue | Main open issue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A / Aperture | Open, precise, architectural | Lowercase, medium weight, custom t/i | Moves beyond an initial while retaining geometric continuity | Bracket/custody/software resemblance; refine its specific proportions |
| B / Fold | Decisive, structural, corporate | Title-case compact grotesk | Bold reproduction and a confident financial-technology presence | Can read as 7, a flag or a generic folded arrow |
| C / Ligature | Measured, editorial, established | Spaced uppercase | A formal signature for research and publishing | TC may imply another name; joins are least clear at 16 px |

These are different strategic characters, not color variants. A is the best fit for the stated parent-brand brief. B is a credible alternative if the desired center of gravity is financial technology. C deliberately tests a more formal publishing position, but its monogram is less distinctive.

## Lettering provenance

- A: Manrope at weight 560 supplies n/c/a outlines. Both lowercase t forms and the square i are drawn specifically for this study; pair spacing is adjusted. The custom t maintains the source x-height, with an upright stem, compact shoulder and squared release.
- B: Instrument Sans at weight 560, title case, with custom pair spacing. This is a type-led direction requiring further terminal work if selected.
- C: Instrument Sans at weight 500, uppercase, with explicit tracking and pair corrections. This is a type-led direction requiring a tighter navigation variant if selected.

The original variable font files and SIL Open Font License notices are included in [type-sources](type-sources/Manrope-OFL.txt). They were obtained from the Google Fonts repository through its jsDelivr mirror: [Manrope source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope), [Instrument Sans source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/instrumentsans). The review loads no remote fonts. It uses SVG paths, not installed font substitution. “Bespoke alphabet” is not claimed for these studies.

## Refinement path after selection

1. Pick one route by character and intended positioning. Avoid combining three unrelated ideas.
2. Draw three close variants of that route. For Aperture, vary opening width, asymmetry and the curved-to-square relationship while retaining its two-part open field.
3. Resolve the lettering and kerning as one word: t–i, i–n, n–c, c–t and t–a. Review large specimens and 88/96/144 px wordmarks.
4. Compare at 16/20/24/32/48 px, in monochrome, reverse, low-quality print and wallet-style tiles. Finalize separate small and reversed masters only after optical inspection.
5. Run a broader visual similarity search and quick unprompted recognition checks with people unfamiliar with the project. The current preliminary screen is not worldwide clearance.
6. Establish clear space, minimum sizes, a quiet Rewards descriptor, accessible names and a canonical geometry source. Only then update the generator and app derivatives, with the existing cross-app visual gates.
