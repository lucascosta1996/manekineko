01 / THE DIRECTIONS

Three different characters.

Read them first in one color. Each route includes a glyph, lettering, a small-size drawing and a product-family treatment. These are proposals for discussion; none is an approved identity.

A / Aperture

Initial recommendation

Open. Precise. Architectural.

A parent identity for research, code and participation.

Two offset brackets define one open field. The space between them is as deliberate as the ink: a compact metaphor for rules that leave room for participation.

Lettering
Lowercase, medium weight, open counters. Custom upright t forms and square i dot, with Manrope-derived n/c/a outlines and optical spacing.
Why consider it
Strongest balance of institutional restraint and Tincta’s geometric art heritage. No standalone letter silhouette.
Watch closely
Offset brackets are a familiar design vocabulary. Review against software, custody and infrastructure marks; avoid adding a containing square or linking it into a chain.

B / Fold

Alternative

Decisive. Structural. Forward.

The strongest direction for a financial technology company.

A broad horizontal plane and an upright plane meet across a diagonal cut. The fold suggests a published edition becoming a structure.

Lettering
Title case, compact grotesk, moderate weight. Instrument Sans outlines with custom pair spacing, led by the flat capital T.
Why consider it
Bold silhouette, very simple reproduction and a clear move toward a corporate identity.
Watch closely
May read as a 7, flag or folded arrow. Angular finance and exchange logos are crowded; the cut and proportions need further differentiation.

C / Ligature

Selected

Measured. Editorial. Established.

A research publisher with a restrained institutional voice.

A compact T/C monogram pairs the opening initial with the central consonant. A shared top edge brings the two letters into one editorial signature.

Lettering
Spaced uppercase with a moderate stroke. Instrument Sans outlines with revised T/I, C/T and T/A spacing.
Why consider it
The most editorial direction; works naturally on research covers and formal communications.
Watch closely
TC can imply two words, a consultancy or a fashion house. Less distinctive and less connected to participation than Aperture; monogram complexity is weakest at 16 px.

05 / THE RESEARCH

Learn the discipline.
Find a different shape.

A cross-category sample covers centralized exchanges, spot and derivatives DEXs, aggregators, lending, staking, stablecoins, yield and infrastructure. It is deliberately broader than the project’s immediate category, without claiming to survey every brand in the market.

Make one visual idea memorable.

Coinbase, Chainlink and OKX use highly reduced forms. Their specific shapes are occupied territory. The useful lesson is consistency and a silhouette that survives reduction.

Connect symbol and lettering.

Aave’s ghost and rounded wordmark feel related. Morpho connects an abstract butterfly with a precise type system. Tincta needs that same internal coherence, with its own source idea.

Separate permanence from expression.

Stripe keeps a stable wordmark amid expressive presentation. Tincta can keep a neutral parent mark while its existing collection artwork carries the color and variation.

Refinement lives in the details.

IBM documents distinct optical treatment for positive and reversed masters. Gap widths, counters and alignment matter as much as the initial concept.

Primary guidance: Coinbase · Aave · Morpho · Chainlink · Pendle · IBM

Why the current glyph invites the Tumblr comparison. The drawings differ, but both are read quickly as a lowercase t: upright, crossbar, then a foot turning to the right. Changing a radius or adding a new color would preserve that dominant reading. A new compact silhouette addresses the concern more directly.

The available territory. A restrained abstract mark drawn from Tincta’s relationship between a defined structure and an open field, paired with legible, moderately weighted lettering. It should feel credible on a research report and recognizable in a wallet-sized tile without relying on gradients or crypto shorthand.

Open the resemblance review and discarded territories

A / Aperture. Check against Ledger and software/custody identities using corner brackets. Keep the two open, offset forms and avoid a four-corner frame. Review the two-part reading against Bitget; the forms should remain brackets rather than opposing arrows.

B / Fold. Check against folded financial monograms such as Nexo, plus generic flags, sevens and exchange arrows. The single diagonal seam must remain distinct without becoming a familiar ribbon N.

C / Ligature. The immediate risk is an anonymous TC consultancy or fashion monogram. Review it against uppercase identity systems and test whether viewers infer a different name.

Across all routes. Avoid Chainlink-like hexagons, Binance-like diamonds, OKX-like grid crosses, and Linear-like striped circles. Literal T explorations also enter the territory of Tether, Tezos and Tumblr. These five additional sources were checked as controls; Tether’s homepage capture was blank, so its official media guide was consulted, not counted in the 30-reference matrix.

The glyphs are newly drawn proposals. This visual screen cannot establish worldwide uniqueness or trademark availability. The selected route still needs a broader similarity search and a final optical pass before adoption.

06 / A PATH FORWARD

Initial recommendation: A / Aperture. The user subsequently selected C / Ligature. Aperture moves furthest from the lowercase initial, works as a quiet parent mark, and leaves room for Tincta’s geometric artwork to remain expressive.

  1. Choose the character

    Select Aperture, Fold or Ligature by the identity it gives the project. Do not combine all three ideas into one mark.

  2. Refine one route

    Develop three close constructions of the selected glyph, then resolve lettering, spacing and the relationship between symbol and name.

  3. Resolve the small details

    Review 16/20/24/32 px, positive and reversed masters, grayscale, print, wallet tiles and neighboring brand silhouettes. Run recognition checks with unfamiliar viewers.

  4. Build the identity system

    After selection, finalize master vectors, clear space, minimum sizes, Rewards lockups and generated derivatives for the existing apps.
