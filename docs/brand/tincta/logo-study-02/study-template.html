05 / THE RESEARCH

Learn the discipline.
Find a different shape.

A cross-category sample covers centralized exchanges, spot and derivatives DEXs, aggregators, lending, staking, stablecoins, yield and infrastructure. It is deliberately broader than the project’s immediate category, without claiming to survey every brand in the market.

Make one visual idea memorable.

Coinbase, Chainlink and OKX use highly reduced forms. Their specific shapes are occupied territory. The useful lesson is consistency and a silhouette that survives reduction.

Connect symbol and lettering.

Aave’s ghost and rounded wordmark feel related. Morpho connects an abstract butterfly with a precise type system. Tincta needs that same internal coherence, with its own source idea.

Separate permanence from expression.

Stripe keeps a stable wordmark amid expressive presentation. Tincta can keep a neutral parent mark while its existing collection artwork carries the color and variation.

Refinement lives in the details.

IBM documents distinct optical treatment for positive and reversed masters. Gap widths, counters and alignment matter as much as the initial concept.

Why the current glyph invites the Tumblr comparison. The drawings differ, but both are read quickly as a lowercase t: upright, crossbar, then a foot turning to the right. The available territory. A restrained abstract mark drawn from Tincta’s relationship between a defined structure and an open field, paired with legible, moderately weighted lettering. It should feel credible on a research report and recognizable in a wallet-sized tile without relying on gradients or crypto shorthand.

06 / A PATH FORWARD

Initial recommendation: A / Aperture. The user subsequently selected C / Ligature. Aperture moves furthest from the lowercase initial, works as a quiet parent mark, and leaves room for Tincta’s geometric artwork to remain expressive.

  1. Choose the character

    Select Aperture, Fold or Ligature by the identity it gives the project. Do not combine all three ideas into one mark.

  2. Refine one route

    Develop three close constructions of the selected glyph, then resolve lettering, spacing and the relationship between symbol and name.

  3. Resolve the small details

    Review 16/20/24/32 px, positive and reversed masters, grayscale, print, wallet tiles and neighboring brand silhouettes. Run recognition checks with unfamiliar viewers.

  4. Build the identity system

    After selection, finalize master vectors, clear space, minimum sizes, Rewards lockups and generated derivatives for the existing apps.
