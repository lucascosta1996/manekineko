DAY 2 — how-it-works
Image: day-02-how-it-works-4k.png

Mint. Sell out. Draw. Claim.

Sellout unlocks the draw. After VRF fulfillment and finalization, winning NFT holders can claim directly from the contract. Draw progression needs transactions; claims need the holder’s wallet action.


DAY 3 — refund-rights
Image: day-03-refund-rights-4k.png

Unsold at the deadline? Current NFT holders can claim the original mint price directly, without owner approval.

Refunded NFTs are burned. Gas and secondary-market purchase prices are not refunded. This refund applies only to collections that expire unsold.


DAY 4 — six-prizes
Image: day-04-six-prizes-4k.png

Planned default: 1,000 NFTs at 0.01 ETH. After sellout and the draw, six winning NFTs receive 1 ETH each.

One wallet may hold multiple winners. Minting does not guarantee a prize. Review the final collection terms before participating.


DAY 5 — direct-claims
Image: day-05-direct-claims-4k.png

Your prize. Your claim.

Once the draw is finalized, the current winning NFT holder claims directly from the contract. No owner approval is required for the claim.

The holder submits a wallet transaction.


DAY 6 — read-the-rules
Image: day-06-read-the-rules-4k.png

Before minting, check the collection’s mint price, supply, deadline, prize allocation and refund conditions.

Verify the network and contract address through official project channels. Read the actual collection terms; planned defaults may vary.
